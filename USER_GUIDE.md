# ACHATPRO – USER GUIDE

## 1. Introduction

ACHATPRO is an international shopping marketplace designed to help users discover products, compare offers, access discounts, and connect with external merchants.

The platform also provides vendor opportunities and marketplace services.

## 2. Creating an Account

Users may register by providing the required information.

Users must provide accurate information and protect their account credentials.

## 3. Buyer Experience

Buyers may:

- Explore product categories.
- Discover recommended products.
- View promotional offers.
- Access discount opportunities.
- Follow affiliate links to external merchants.

## 4. Product Discovery

Users may discover products through categories, recommendations, promotional listings, and available search features.

Product availability and offers depend on the relevant merchant.

## 5. Purchasing Products

ACHATPRO operates primarily through an affiliate marketplace model.

When users select an affiliate product, they may be redirected to the merchant's website to complete their purchase.

The merchant generally manages checkout, payment, shipping, and delivery for affiliate purchases.

## 6. Discounts and Recommendations

ACHATPRO may provide promotional offers, recommendations, and discounted product listings.

Offers may vary depending on merchants and promotional campaigns.

## 7. Vendor Registration

Interested vendors may submit a registration request.

Vendor registration is subject to ACHATPRO's applicable verification and approval requirements.

## 8. Vendor Product Management

Approved vendors may submit product information, photographs, descriptions, prices, and external purchase links when applicable.

Products may be reviewed before publication.

## 9. Vendor Responsibilities

Vendors must:

- Provide accurate product information.
- Use authentic product photographs.
- Respect intellectual property rights.
- Maintain reliable contact information.
- Follow platform policies.
- Respect applicable marketplace requirements.

## 10. Commission System

ACHATPRO may generate revenue through affiliate commissions and authorized marketplace services.

The standard platform commission rate is 10%, subject to applicable transaction conditions.

## 11. Free and Pro Services

ACHATPRO may offer free and paid services.

Service features, subscription prices, eligibility, and limits are subject to the official platform terms.

## 12. Payments

Payments on external merchant websites are managed according to the merchant's payment policies.

Payments processed directly through ACHATPRO, when available, are subject to applicable payment procedures.

## 13. Delivery

For affiliate purchases, the external merchant generally manages shipping and delivery.

For transactions directly managed through ACHATPRO, delivery responsibilities depend on the applicable transaction arrangement.

## 14. Security

Users must protect their accounts and provide accurate information.

ACHATPRO may apply verification procedures to protect users and the platform.

## 15. Support

Users and vendors may contact ACHATPRO through officially provided support channels.

## 16. Important Notice

Some services, integrations, payment methods, promotional features, and marketplace functionalities may depend on implementation, availability, and applicable platform policies.
