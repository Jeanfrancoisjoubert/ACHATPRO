# ACHATPRO - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. What is ACHATPRO?

ACHATPRO is an international shopping marketplace designed to help buyers discover products, compare offers, access discounts, and connect with merchants.

## 2. How do I create an account?

Users may register through the platform by providing the required information and following the registration process.

## 3. Is registration free?

Buyer registration is free, subject to the platform's applicable terms.

## 4. How can I find products?

Users may explore product categories, recommendations, promotional listings, and available product searches.

## 5. How do I purchase a product?

When a product is available through an affiliate link, users may be redirected to the merchant's website to complete their purchase.

## 6. Does ACHATPRO directly sell all products?

No. ACHATPRO operates primarily as an affiliate marketplace. Purchases may be completed directly on external merchant websites.

## 7. How do discounts work?

Discounts and promotional offers depend on the relevant merchant, campaign, product, and applicable conditions.

## 8. How can I become a vendor?

Interested vendors may submit a registration request. Vendor accounts are subject to platform review and approval.

## 9. Is vendor registration free?

Vendor registration is free, subject to the platform's applicable rules.

## 10. How can vendors publish products?

Approved vendors may submit product information, images, descriptions, prices, and links for review and publication.

## 11. Are all products automatically approved?

No. ACHATPRO may review, approve, reject, or request additional information about submitted products.

## 12. How does ACHATPRO earn revenue?

ACHATPRO may generate revenue through affiliate commissions and other authorized platform services.

## 13. What is the standard commission rate?

The standard platform commission rate is 10%, subject to applicable transaction conditions and commission policies.

## 14. How do Free and Pro plans work?

ACHATPRO may provide Free and paid subscription services. Features, limits, pricing, and eligibility depend on the applicable plan terms.

## 15. How can I contact support?

Users and vendors may contact ACHATPRO through the official support channels made available by the platform.

## 16. What should I do if a product link does not work?

Users should report the problem through the available support channels. Product links may depend on the external merchant's availability.

## 17. Who is responsible for delivery?

For purchases completed on external merchant websites, delivery is generally managed by the relevant merchant.

## 18. Can I request a refund?

Refunds, returns, and cancellations depend on the merchant's applicable policies for external purchases or the rules applicable to transactions directly managed by ACHATPRO.

## 19. How does ACHATPRO protect users?

ACHATPRO seeks to implement appropriate measures for account security, information protection, platform administration, and verification.

## 20. Can ACHATPRO change its services?

Yes. ACHATPRO may update its features, services, policies, plans, and platform operations when necessary.
