# ACHATPRO - Official Platform Policies

## 1. Platform Overview

ACHATPRO is an international marketplace discovery and affiliate platform designed to connect buyers with products, vendors, discounts, and external merchant stores.

ACHATPRO provides product discovery, promotional information, recommendations, and authorized marketplace services.

## 2. Platform Usage

Users agree to use ACHATPRO responsibly and in compliance with applicable laws and platform rules.

Users must not:
- Use the platform for fraudulent activities.
- Submit false information.
- Attempt unauthorized access.
- Manipulate promotional systems.
- Abuse referral or recommendation features.
- Upload prohibited or misleading content.

## 3. Account Information and Security

Users are responsible for providing accurate information when registration or account identification is required.

Users must protect their account credentials and report unauthorized account access when discovered.

ACHATPRO may restrict or suspend accounts involved in suspected abuse, fraud, or violations of platform rules.

## 4. Marketplace and Affiliate Model

ACHATPRO operates primarily through an affiliate marketplace and product-redirection model.

Products displayed on ACHATPRO may be sold and fulfilled by external merchants.

When buyers click product links, they may be redirected to external merchant websites to complete purchases.

ACHATPRO does not automatically become the seller of products listed by external merchants.

## 5. External Merchant Responsibilities

External merchants are responsible for:
- Product availability.
- Product quality.
- Product descriptions and specifications.
- Pricing and applicable taxes.
- Payment processing on their own websites.
- Order confirmation.
- Shipping and delivery.
- Returns and refunds, according to their policies.
- Customer service concerning external purchases.

## 6. Product Information and Promotions

ACHATPRO may display product descriptions, prices, discounts, recommendations, and promotional information supplied by vendors, merchants, or authorized sources.

Prices, promotional offers, availability, and merchant conditions may change without prior notice.

Users should verify the final price, shipping costs, availability, and applicable conditions on the merchant website before completing a purchase.

## 7. Affiliate Commissions

ACHATPRO may earn affiliate commissions when eligible purchases are completed through authorized affiliate links.

Affiliate commissions are subject to merchant tracking systems, merchant validation, applicable affiliate agreements, and transaction eligibility.

ACHATPRO does not guarantee that every redirected purchase will generate a commission.

## 8. Vendor Participation

Vendor participation is subject to registration requirements, platform verification, and approval when required.

Vendors must provide accurate product information, valid links, and appropriate contact information.

ACHATPRO reserves the right to reject, suspend, or remove vendors or product listings that violate platform policies.

## 9. Vendor Verification and Product Review

ACHATPRO may request:
- Vendor identification information.
- Vendor photographs.
- Product photographs.
- Contact information.
- Additional verification documents.

Verification requirements may vary according to platform operations and risk assessments.

## 10. Discounts and Recommendations

ACHATPRO may provide product recommendations, discount listings, promotional campaigns, and other discovery tools.

Recommendations and promotional listings do not constitute a guarantee of product quality, merchant reliability, or price availability.

Promotional conditions may vary according to the relevant merchant or campaign.

## 11. Plans and Platform Services

ACHATPRO may provide free and paid services, subscription plans, vendor tools, promotional features, and other platform benefits.

Service limits, subscription prices, features, and eligibility may be updated when necessary.

Any paid subscription must be subject to clearly communicated terms.

## 12. Payments and Transactions

Payments completed on external merchant websites are processed according to the relevant merchant's payment policies.

Payments processed directly through ACHATPRO, when available, are subject to the applicable payment procedures and transaction rules.

ACHATPRO may restrict or investigate transactions suspected of fraud or abuse.

## 13. Delivery and Order Fulfillment

For affiliate purchases completed on external merchant websites, the external merchant is generally responsible for order fulfillment, shipping, delivery, and customer service.

For transactions directly managed through ACHATPRO, delivery responsibilities depend on the applicable transaction arrangements and seller responsibilities.

## 14. Refunds and Returns

Refunds, cancellations, and returns for purchases completed on external merchant websites are generally governed by the relevant merchant's policies.

For transactions directly managed through ACHATPRO, refund procedures depend on the applicable transaction rules and payment arrangements.

## 15. Intellectual Property

Users and vendors must respect intellectual property rights.

Unauthorized use of trademarks, copyrighted materials, images, content, or protected information may result in removal of content or account restrictions.

## 16. Privacy and Information Protection

ACHATPRO may collect and process information necessary for platform operations, account management, verification, security, and authorized services.

Personal information should be handled according to applicable privacy requirements and the platform's privacy practices.

ACHATPRO must take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

## 17. Platform Availability

ACHATPRO may experience interruptions, technical limitations, maintenance periods, or service changes.

The platform does not guarantee uninterrupted availability of all services, merchant links, promotions, or integrations.

## 18. Suspension and Restriction

ACHATPRO reserves the right to restrict, suspend, or terminate access when users or vendors violate platform rules, engage in fraudulent activity, misuse services, or create security risks.

## 19. Policy Updates

ACHATPRO may update these policies to reflect operational changes, technical improvements, new services, or legal requirements.

Updated policies may be published on the platform.

## 20. Contact and Assistance

Users and vendors may contact ACHATPRO through the support channels officially provided by the platform for assistance, complaints, account questions, or operational inquiries.

---

This document establishes general platform policies. Specific legal, financial, privacy, consumer protection, and operational requirements may require additional policies according to the countries in which ACHATPRO operates.
