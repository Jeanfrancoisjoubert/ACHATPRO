# ACHATPRO - Buyer Guide

## 1. Overview

ACHATPRO is an international marketplace designed to help buyers discover products, compare offers, access discounts, and connect with external merchants.

## 2. Buyer Registration

Buyers may create an account to access available marketplace features.

Users should provide accurate information during registration and protect their account credentials.

## 3. Product Discovery

Buyers may explore products through marketplace categories, recommendations, promotional listings, and product searches when available.

## 4. Product Information

Product listings may include:

- Product name.
- Product images.
- Product description.
- Price.
- Discount information.
- Merchant information.
- External purchase link.

Buyers should review product information before proceeding with a purchase.

## 5. Recommendations

ACHATPRO may recommend products based on marketplace promotional activities, discounts, product availability, and other platform features.

## 6. Discounts and Promotions

ACHATPRO may display discounted products and promotional offers.

Discounts are subject to the terms and conditions of the relevant merchant or promotional campaign.

## 7. Affiliate Redirection

ACHATPRO operates through an affiliate marketplace model.

When buyers select a product, they may be redirected to an external merchant website to complete their purchase.

The external merchant may control the final checkout process, payment, order confirmation, shipping, and delivery.

## 8. Payment

Payments for products purchased through external merchant websites are generally processed by the relevant merchant according to its payment policies.

ACHATPRO payment functionality may also be available for supported platform transactions, subject to implementation and availability.

## 9. Orders

Order confirmation, order tracking, cancellation, refunds, and customer service may depend on the external merchant when the purchase is completed outside ACHATPRO.

Buyers should retain their order confirmation and payment information.

## 10. Delivery

Delivery is generally managed by the merchant or seller responsible for fulfilling the order.

Delivery costs, delivery times, tracking information, and shipping restrictions depend on the relevant merchant.

## 11. Buyer Responsibilities

Buyers are responsible for:

- Reviewing product information.
- Confirming prices before purchasing.
- Providing accurate delivery information.
- Reviewing merchant policies.
- Protecting their account credentials.
- Contacting the appropriate merchant when necessary.

## 12. Security

Buyers should use secure connections and avoid sharing account credentials or payment information with unauthorized individuals.

## 13. Support

ACHATPRO may provide assistance regarding platform navigation, product discovery, account access, recommendations, and available marketplace features.

For purchases completed on external merchant websites, buyers may need to contact the merchant directly.
