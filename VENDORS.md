# ACHATPRO - Vendor Management System

## 1. Overview

ACHATPRO is an international marketplace that connects vendors and buyers.
The platform allows vendors to register, submit products, manage their listings, and generate sales through affiliate-based marketplace redirection.

## 2. Vendor Registration

- Vendor registration is free.
- Vendor accounts require approval.
- Vendors must provide accurate registration information.
- Identity verification may be required.
- Vendors must provide a valid contact method.

## 3. Vendor Verification

ACHATPRO may request:

- Vendor profile information.
- Identity verification document.
- Photo of the vendor.
- Product photos.
- Additional verification information when necessary.

Vendor approval is subject to platform review.

## 4. Product Management

Approved vendors may:

- Submit products for review.
- Upload product images.
- Provide product descriptions.
- Submit product prices.
- Provide product links.
- Update product information when authorized.

Products may require verification before publication.

## 5. Product Approval

Products submitted by vendors must comply with ACHATPRO platform rules.

ACHATPRO reserves the right to:

- Approve products.
- Reject products.
- Request additional information.
- Suspend product listings that violate platform policies.

## 6. Affiliate Marketplace Model

ACHATPRO operates using an affiliate redirection model.

Buyers may discover products on ACHATPRO and proceed to the merchant's website to complete their purchases.

ACHATPRO may generate revenue through affiliate commissions and other authorized platform services.

## 7. Vendor Responsibilities

Vendors are responsible for:

- Providing accurate product information.
- Maintaining valid product links.
- Respecting applicable marketplace policies.
- Responding to verification requests.
- Maintaining accurate contact information.

## 8. Commission Policy

ACHATPRO's standard platform commission rate is 10%.

Commission calculations are subject to applicable transaction tracking and confirmation rules.

No commission is considered earned when:

- The transaction is cancelled.
- Payment is not confirmed.
- The product has not been delivered when delivery confirmation is required.

## 9. Vendor Plans

### FREE PLAN

- Free registration.
- Maximum of 20 products.
- Access to basic marketplace features.

### PRO PLAN

- Subscription price: $14.99 USD per month.
- Access to additional vendor features according to platform availability.

## 10. Vendor Responsibilities Regarding Delivery

Vendors or the relevant merchants are responsible for product fulfillment and delivery according to the applicable commercial arrangements.

ACHATPRO does not automatically assume responsibility for physical product delivery.

## 11. Discounts and Recommendations

ACHATPRO may promote products through:

- Discount campaigns.
- Product recommendations.
- Marketplace visibility features.
- Promotional activities.

These features may depend on platform eligibility and availability.

## 12. Account Security

Vendors must protect their account credentials.

ACHATPRO may suspend accounts involved in fraud, abuse, misleading information, or unauthorized activity.

## 13. Platform Rules

Vendors must comply with ACHATPRO policies.

ACHATPRO may review, restrict, suspend, or remove vendor accounts or products when necessary to protect buyers and platform integrity.

## 14. Support

Vendors may contact ACHATPRO support for assistance with registration, verification, product submissions, account access, or platform operations.
